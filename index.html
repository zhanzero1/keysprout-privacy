KeySprout privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

KeySprout is a Windows app that teaches children to touch type and to spell. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: KeySprout does not collect any personal information.

What KeySprout stores

KeySprout saves the following on the computer it is installed on, and nowhere else:

  • the first name or nickname typed for each player, and the picture chosen;
  • lesson results, stars, typing accuracy and speed, and which keys were missed;
  • spelling lists that a parent or child types in, and which words have been learned;
  • settings such as sound and keyboard layout.

This information stays in the app's own folder on the computer. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a player in the Grown-ups area, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

What KeySprout does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No use of the internet for lessons, spelling or the game. KeySprout does not record what is typed outside the app.
  • No links out of the app.

Purchases

The full version is bought through the Microsoft Store. When a grown-up opens the Full version page or makes a purchase, Windows contacts the Microsoft Store to show the price and handle the payment. Microsoft processes that purchase under its own privacy statement. KeySprout never sees payment details.

Parts of Windows that KeySprout uses

KeySprout shows its screens with Microsoft Edge WebView2, a part of Windows made by Microsoft. KeySprout switches off that component's background updates and crash reports, and gives it nothing to send: no names, no progress and no word lists. Like other parts of Windows, it may still contact Microsoft on its own account, under Microsoft's privacy statement.

Words in the listen-and-type test are read aloud by a voice installed on the computer. KeySprout does not use online voices.

Children

KeySprout is designed for children and collects no personal information from them or from anyone else. Purchases sit behind a question intended for adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
